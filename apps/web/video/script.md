# OpsSlate Product Launch Video Script
# Duration: ~90 seconds
# Style: Fast-paced, punchy, dark theme matching app

## SCENE 1: The Hook (0-8s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** "Your PM software costs HOW much?"
**NARRATION:** "Procore charges fifty thousand dollars a year. And your field crews still hate using it."

## SCENE 2: The Problem (8-18s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** Problem bullets appearing one by one
**NARRATION:** "Daily logs get pencil-whipped at 4 PM Friday. Foremen aren't data entry clerks. And no PM tool uses AI. Until now."

## SCENE 3: Intro (18-25s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** OpsSlate logo + "Your Operations. One Slate."
**NARRATION:** "Introducing OpsSlate. AI-powered construction project management. Twenty plus modules. Forty nine dollars a month."

## SCENE 4: Voice Daily Logs (25-35s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** 🎙️ Voice-to-Daily-Log feature
**NARRATION:** "Talk to your phone. AI writes the daily log. Thirty seconds. Done. No typing. No forms. No excuses."

## SCENE 5: AI Autopilot (35-45s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** 🤖 AI Autopilot dashboard
**NARRATION:** "AI Autopilot analyzes your entire project. Crew, budget, weather, safety. Then tells you exactly what needs attention. Like having a junior PM watching twenty four seven."

## SCENE 6: Health Scores (45-52s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** 📊 Project health scores
**NARRATION:** "Every project gets a health score. Zero to one hundred. Red, yellow, green. Open the app, know exactly where to focus."

## SCENE 7: Feature Montage (52-65s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** Quick cuts of features
**NARRATION:** "Punch lists. Change orders. Budget tracking. RFIs. Submittals. Safety incidents. Weather intelligence. Crew management. Time tracking. Document management. All connected. All real-time."

## SCENE 8: The Comparison (65-75s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** Comparison table (OpsSlate vs Procore)
**NARRATION:** "Eight features Procore doesn't have. One hundred times cheaper. Real-time data instead of stale refreshes. And AI that actually thinks."

## SCENE 9: CTA (75-90s)
**TEXT ON SCREEN:** opsslate.app + "Start Free"
**NARRATION:** "Stop overpaying for dumb software. OpsSlate. Free to start. No credit card. The future of construction management is here. ops slate dot app."
